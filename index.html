Operating Regime Detection

Automated classification of pump operating states using PCA score thresholds

✅
Normal Operation
Stable equilibrium zone
—
  • |PC1 Score|< 1.5σ
  • Solar Irradiance600–1000 W/m²
  • Flow Rate40–80 L/min
  • Vibration< 2.5g
System operates within designed parameters. Solar panels deliver rated output, pump runs at optimal RPM, water levels are stable.
⚠️
Strained Operation
Elevated stress zone
—
  • |PC1 Score|1.5–3.0σ
  • Solar Irradiance< 600 W/m²
  • Flow Rate< 40 or >80
  • Vibration2.5–5.0g
Solar output degraded (cloud cover / panel fault). Pump compensates, increasing load. Vibration rises — intervention recommended.
🚨
Anomaly Detected
Critical out-of-distribution
—
  • |PC1 Score|> 3.0σ
  • T² Statistic> 99th pct
  • Q Statistic> 95th pct
  • Vibration> 5.0g
Critical deviation from operational envelope. Potential equipment failure, blockage, or sensor malfunction. Immediate inspection required.
